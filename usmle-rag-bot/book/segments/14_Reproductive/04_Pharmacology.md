[Section: High-Yield Organ Systems | Topic: Reproductive | Subtopic: Pharmacology | Heading: **Anabolic (androgenic) steroids**]
#### **Anabolic (androgenic) steroids**  
|MECHANISM|Exogenous synthetic testosterone derivatives bind androgen receptor in muscle→ ↑protein<br>synthesis, erythropoiesis, muscle mass; binding of glucocorticoid receptors inhibits muscle<br>breakdown.<br>Negative feedback→ ↓GnRH→ ↓LH/FSH→ ↓endogenous testosterone, spermatogenesis.|
|---|---|
|PERFORMANCE EFFECTS|↑lean mass, strength, recovery; no true↑aerobic capacity.|
|ADVERSE EFFECTS|Hepatotoxicity (↑AST/ALT, cholestasis, hepatic adenoma), polycythemia (↑EPO).<br>Psychiatric: aggression (“roid rage”), mania, withdrawal depression; mood swings common.<br>Cardiovascular:↑LDL,↓HDL→ ↑atherosclerosis/MI risk.<br>Males: Testicular atrophy, infertility (↓spermatogenesis), gynecomastia (↑aromatization→ ↑E2),<br>acne, premature epiphyseal closure (young users).<br>Females: Virilization(hirsutism, amenorrhea, clitoromegaly),↓breast size, acne, alopecia.|
|ENDOCRINE FEEDBACK|Chronic use→ ↓GnRH→ ↓LH/FSH→ ↓intratesticular T→azoospermia, infertility.<br>Recoveryslowpost-discontinuation.|
|NOTES|Suspect usage in young male with infertility + high muscle mass + small testes + gynecomastia<br>(not a side effect with stanozolol use due to fusion of A ring, no aromatization can occur).|
