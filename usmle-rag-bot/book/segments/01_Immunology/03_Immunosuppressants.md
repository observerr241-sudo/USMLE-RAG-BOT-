## ▶ IMMUNOLOGY—IMMUNOSUPPRESSANTS  
**Immunosuppressants** Agents that block lymphocyte activation and proliferation. Reduce acute transplant rejection by suppressing cellular immunity (used as prophylaxis). Frequently combined to achieve greater efficacy with ↓ toxicity. Chronic suppression ↑ risk of infection and malignancy.
