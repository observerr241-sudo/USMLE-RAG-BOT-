**Anterior pituitary** Secretes FSH, LH, ACTH, TSH, prolactin, **Pro** opiomelanocortin (POMC) derivatives— **(adenohypophysis)** GH, and β-endorphin. Melanotropin (MSH) β-endorphin, **A** CTH, and **M** SH. Go **pro** with secreted from intermediate lobe of pituitary. a **BAM** ! Derived from **neuro** ectoderm.
