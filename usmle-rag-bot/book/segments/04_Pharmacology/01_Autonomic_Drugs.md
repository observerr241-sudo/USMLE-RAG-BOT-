**Acetylcholine** Nicotinic ACh receptors are ligand-gated channels allowing efflux of K<sup>+</sup> and influx of Na<sup>+</sup> and in **receptors** some cases Ca<sup>2+</sup> . Two subtypes: NN (found in autonomic ganglia, adrenal medulla) and NM (found in neuromuscular junction of skeletal muscle).
