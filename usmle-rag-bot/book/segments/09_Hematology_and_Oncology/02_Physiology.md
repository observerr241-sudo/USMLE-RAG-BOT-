### **Antiglobulin test**  
Also called Coombs test. Detects the presence of antibodies against circulating RBCs. **Direct** antiglobulin test—anti-human globulin (Coombs reagent) added to patient’s RBCs. RBCs agglutinate if RBCs are ( **directly** ) coated with anti-RBC Abs. Used for AIHA diagnosis. Indirect ( **not** direct) antiglobulin test—normal RBCs added to patient’s serum. If serum has antiRBC Abs, RBCs agglutinate when Coombs reagent is added. Used for pretransfusion testing.
