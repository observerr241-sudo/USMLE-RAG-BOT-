### **Major medical insurance plans**  
|**Exclusive provider**<br>**organization (EPO)**|Requires in-network care; out-of-network coverage available for emergencies.|
|---|---|
|**Health maintenance**<br>**organization (HMO)**|Lower premiums, limited to in-network providers, requires primary care referral for specialists,<br>lower out-of-pocket cost.|
|**Point of service (PoS)**|Combination of HMO and PPO, allows out-of-network care at higher cost, and requires primary<br>care referrals.|
|**Preferred provider**<br>**organization (PPO)**|Flexibility with in-network and out-of-network care, higher cost for out-of-network services, no<br>referral requirement.|
|**Accountable care**<br>**organization (ACO)**|Group of providers who voluntarily coordinate care for Medicare patients.|
|**High deductible**<br>**health plan (HDHP)**|Low premiums, high out-of-pocket cost, compatible with health savings account (HSA).|
